Privacy Policy

A plain-language starting template for how a business website may handle enquiry information.

Important: This is a starter template, not legal advice. Review it with a qualified professional and update it to reflect the actual tools, laws, and data practices used by HerNest Solutions before publishing.

Information you provide

If you contact us, you may choose to share your name, business name, email address, country, and project details. Only request information needed to respond to your enquiry.

How information may be used

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, discuss requested services, and maintain business communications. The final policy should describe any analytics, hosting, CRM, email, or other third-party services actually in use.

Sharing and retention

Do not sell personal information. Explain any service providers that process data on the business’s behalf, how long information is retained, and the circumstances in which disclosure may be required by law.

Your choices

Provide a verified contact method for privacy questions and explain the rights and choices available to visitors under applicable law.

Updates

When this policy changes, update the effective date and describe material changes where appropriate.
